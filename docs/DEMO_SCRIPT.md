# 3-minute demo script

**0:00 · Problem (20 s).** "More drug launches miss expectations than meet them, and limited market access is the
most-cited reason. In 2026, IRA negotiation, MFN pricing, tariffs and PBM reform all change launch economics at once.
LaunchLens puts the five forces that decide a launch into one research workbench, on public data, with AI that has to
cite every number."

**0:20 · Governance (20 s).** Show sign-up → pending (24-hour SLA, auto-approval) → first login forces AI setup: own
key unlimited, shared key capped by admin policy.

**0:40 · Command Center (15 s).** Apply the *Oncology · CDK4/6* scope. Point out the tiles: the Pricing tile reads
"3 of 3". Ibrance (IPAY 2027), Kisqali and Verzenio (IPAY 2028) are all selected for Medicare negotiation.

**0:55 · R2 Pricing (25 s).** Negotiation Tracker (★ scope drugs, CMS-cited MFPs), then Candidate Radar ("estimate,
transparent rules").

**1:20 · R1 Access (20 s).** Access vs. Demand quadrant: "these are the states where we should fix access before
spending on reps."

**1:40 · R3 Policy (15 s).** Radar plus deadlines: "a PBM proposed rule closes comments on this date."

**1:55 · R4 Targeting (25 s).** Move the access weight slider and watch the deciles re-rank, then show the Model Card
backtest lift.

**2:20 · R5 + agent (25 s).** Ask "Who are the Phase 3 entrants and when do they read out?", show the *Citations
verified* badge and the data lineage, then pin the answer.

**2:45 · Brief (15 s).** Generate the executive brief (sources appendix, disclaimer, signature), then print to PDF.
"Architected and built through a full SDLC: specs, ADRs, 39 acceptance tests, security review, CI, Replit deploy."
